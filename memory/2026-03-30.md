## Tender Submission Flow Explained

### Actor: Admin/Project Manager
1. Create tender from project/BOQ
2. Set deadline and validity period
3. Invite contractors
4. Review submissions
5. Accept or reject with notes

### Actor: Contractor
1. Receive tender invitation
2. View BOQ and specifications
3. Submit bid with pricing
4. Receive award/rejection notification
